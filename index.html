Trading research

Two long/short models on XAU/USD and BTC/USD, one daily trend model and one intraday. Each is specified from the literature, backtested net of transaction costs, validated by walk-forward analysis, frozen, and then run on a simulated account. Week 3 of 16

Every trade answers four questions. Each has a written note with the formulas, a lab on simulated series and the sources.

Signal

Which way?

Time-series momentum: long if the price is above where it was a year ago, short otherwise. The mechanism, the choice of lookback and the published evidence.

Time-series momentum
Sizing

How big?

Position equals risk target divided by EWMA volatility, so gold at 18% volatility and bitcoin at 64% carry the same expected risk in one book.

Volatility by EWMA
Testing

Can I trust the backtest?

Signals use only past data, positions lag one bar, costs and carry are deducted. Four sources of error with a control for each, and a 157-line backtester traced by hand on a six-day example.

Judging

Is the Sharpe ratio real?

The sampling error of the estimate, the inflation from picking the best of many trials, the deflated Sharpe ratio and the multiple-testing haircut.

Sharpe ratio estimation

First run, week 1

12-month time-series momentum sized to a 10% annual volatility target, with spread costs and carry, on daily data since 2018.

Bitcoin Sharpe 0.59 Maximum drawdown −22%, against about −77% for buying and holding.
Gold Sharpe 0.24 After fixing a bug that closed every Friday and reopened every Monday, paying the spread twice a week.

A plumbing check, not a result. Both numbers cover the full history and are not yet out of sample. The plan and the weekly status are in the research plan.

Quantum chemistry research

Computational work from 2022 to 2025. What carries over to trading: compare methods before trusting one, check predictions against measurement, and report how sensitive a result is to its assumptions.

2025
Excited-state dynamics of PALBOX, a strained diazirine photoaffinity tag Northeastern University, Lopez Lab

Compared excitation energies from three levels of theory (TD-DFT, CASSCF and XMS-CASPT2) to establish which could be trusted for the reactive intermediates.

2025
A non-De Mayo photochemical pathway Northeastern University, Lopez Lab

The mechanism of a reaction that departs from a pathway accepted for sixty years: transition-state and TD-DFT calculations set beside the experimental evidence. Slides

2025
Z/E isomerisation of ketone-based hydrazones

Barriers and half-lives for four compounds at M06-2X/6-311++G(d,p). An 11 kcal/mol spread in the barrier moves the half-life from 3.4 seconds to 106.6 years. Slides

2022–2024
Reactions of CH3OH with the OH radical on interstellar ices University of Colombo

Rate constants with Wigner and Eckart tunnelling corrections. The barriers proved sensitive to the number of surrounding molecules, and tunnelling changed which product dominates at low temperature. The predictions were checked against laboratory measurements by an experimental group at Hokkaido University.
